2. 4 Curriculum Modules (Theory vs. Practical Structure)

1
Module 1: Introduction to Agriculture & Soil Science (Theory 📖 • 18 Hours) Foundations of soil horizons (O, A, B, C), soil chemistry (pH, EC, CEC), N-P-K nutrient cycles, and regenerative soil carbon sequestration. Assessed via Comprehensive Theory Quiz.
2
Module 2: Soil Sample Collection & Preparation (Practical 🧪 • 18 Hours) Hands-on field sampling: zig-zag auger extraction at 0-15cm furrow depth, GPS geotagging, composite quartering, shade drying, and 2mm stainless mesh sieving. Assessed via Field Assignment.
3
Module 3: Soil Testing using SoiLENZ Handheld Device (Practical ⚡ • 18 Hours) Operating proprietary optical sensor: two-point buffer calibration (pH 4.0/7.0), quartz cuvette loading, automated 90-second multi-parameter scan. Assessed via SoiLENZ Challenge.
4
Module 4: Soil Health Reports & Farmer Advisory (Practical 🌾 • 18 Hours) Generating Soil Health Cards, calculating customized fertilizer dosage, and student-led vernacular advisory meetings with village farmers. Assessed via Village Advisory Capstone.

5. Strict School Eligibility & Certification Gates

Gate 1: School Eligibility Approval

Schools submit 18 verification points across Space, Infrastructure, Safety, Faculty, Students (80+ registrations), and signed MoU. Requires ≥85% score with 0 critical flags before changing status from "Pending" to "Enrolled".

Gate 2: Soil Partner Certification Gate

Students must achieve: 1) ≥80% attendance rate (≥58 of 72 hours), 2) Pass all 4 modules, 3) Complete hands-on SoiLENZ testing practical, and 4) Complete farmer advisory. Includes tamper-proof QR code verification.
